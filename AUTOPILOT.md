# Deep Frame Motion · daily autopilot

> **AUTOPILOT_V2.md is the master workflow (owner, 7 Nov 2026).** This file holds the technical how-to and house rules. If anything conflicts, V2's safety, religious and licensing rules win. Target: 10 Reels/day, 08:00–21:00, at least 45 min apart.

Each daily run fills the target day up to 10 Reels for Instagram @deepframemotion and schedules them in Metricool for the NEXT day.

## Setup in a fresh session
1. `git clone https://github.com/farisroadto8fig/deepframemotion-videos /home/claude/deepframemotion-videos`
2. `cp -r /home/claude/deepframemotion-videos/studio /home/claude/studio`
3. The engine: `studio/README.md`. Generator template: `studio/videos/tnt_gen.py` (THIS, NOT THAT format, `TNT({...})`, 31 s).
4. Render: `node lib/render.js videos/<dir>/video.html build --kit <kit> --out <Name>.mp4` (run from /home/claude/studio; about 10 min per video, so run 2 at a time).
5. Background sound: `videos/tnt_bgmix.sh <dir>` (removes the synth music bed, adds `audio/calm_bg.m4a` softly, normalises). It expects the output name `ThisNotThat_<NN>_<name>.mp4`. For other names, adapt the script.

## Every run
1. **Analytics.** Metricool brand id `7926528`, timezone `Africa/Casablanca`. Pull Instagram reels metrics for the last 14 days (`getAnalyticsAvailableMetrics` network instagram connector reels, then `getAnalyticsDataByMetrics`). Rank posts by views, then shares and saves. Write the top 3 and bottom 3 topics plus formats into `log.md`.
2. **Pick 4 topics.** Lean towards what works best: the same series format, the same kind of hook and theme. Never repeat a topic listed in `posted.md`.
3. **Verify every hadith and ayah** with a web search (number and wording). No weak or fabricated hadith. Write the source on screen and in the caption.
4. **Build** in the same style, then preview a contact sheet (frames 0, 5, 11.5, 15, 20, 25) and fix overlaps before rendering.
5. **Render, add the background sound, check** with ffprobe (1080x1920, 30 fps, about 31 s, roughly −14 to −16 LUFS).
6. **Upload** the 4 mp4s to the root of this repo and push. Media URL: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/farisroadto8fig/deepframemotion-videos/main/<file>.mp4`
7. **Schedule** in Metricool as Instagram REEL (`autoPublish: true`) at the best times for the next day (`getBestTimeToPostByNetwork`; the usual peaks are about 10:00, 12:30, 16:00 and 18:30). Never schedule in the past, never schedule more than 10 per day, and keep at least 45 min between posts. Each post gets a `firstCommentText` such as "Part N is up next 🤍 Follow so you don't miss it".
8. **Log.** Append the topics and dates to `posted.md` and the stats notes to `log.md`, then push.
9. **Report** to the user in Dutch: what was scheduled and when, what performed best and why, and one idea to grow.

## Sounds (V2 Phase 7)

- Vary the kit per video and match it to the mood (night = calm reflection, airy = morning or hope, organic = everyday life, minimal = focus, digital = modern struggle).

## House rules (always)
- Figures are always faceless: no eyes, mouth or features (halal). Yusuf (kufi, thobe), Maryam (hijab, abaya) and optional Ali/Mum. Maryam stands behind Yusuf in prayer.
- When the text names an action (pray, wuḍūʾ, duʿāʾ, eat…), the figures must actually do it, with matching SFX. Figures walk and move; they never just stand still.
- One text layer only. The hook is visible on the very first frame, gives a clear benefit and is not clickbait.
- No "DF" logo. Captions are in English with "👉 Follow @deepframemotion".
- Sources are verified, and the Arabic, transliteration and translation are correct.
- Never post anything political, sectarian or controversial. Keep it to everyday sunnah, adab, duʿāʾ and reminders.
- Goal: grow the page, so that it can later sell e-books and an e-course ("Deep Frame Motion").

## Growth playbook (owner request, 7 Nov 2026)
Based on research into top creators in the niche (The Sunnah Guy, Itqān, Yaqeen, big faceless pages) and Instagram's own creator guidance. Use **techniques only**: never copy their lines, visuals or audio.
1. **3-part formula (The Sunnah Guy):** a hook in the first 3 s that anyone understands and that makes them curious, one core message with no side tracks, and an ending that **pays off the hook** with one line people want to share as "this is me".
2. **Share line at the end:** the last caption (27–31 s) is a short identity line, e.g. "Be the one who ___." / "Small deed. Every day. Forever." The top tip stays in the tip card.
3. **Hook bank (templates, fill them in yourself):** "One day you will ___." · "You've been doing ___ wrong your whole life." · "Stop ___. Do this instead." · "The Prophet ﷺ never ___. Here's what he did." · "___ seconds is all it takes to earn ___." · "Most people ___ after salah. Very few ___." · "Don't say ___ when ___. Say ___." · "You're losing ___ every time you ___." · "Everyone knows ___. Almost no one knows ___." · "Two phrases, light on the tongue, heavy on ___." Vary them: no more than 2 videos per day with the same pattern.
4. **Sound-off first:** about half of viewers watch without sound, so the hook must work as text plus image on frame 1 (already a house rule).
5. **Ranking signals:** watch time, likes per reach, and **sends per reach** (DM shares, the most important signal for non-followers). Every caption gets one concrete send prompt ("Send this to the one who ___").
6. **Topics:** analytics show concrete dhikr/duʿāʾ + clear reward + exact source gets the most saves. Aim for at least 4 of 10 per day of that type. Metaphor-only reels (Wait For It style) get views but few saves.
7. **Original content:** Instagram ranks original content higher. Our engine-made animation and synth sound count as original. No reposts, no other apps' logos.
8. **Sound:** no trending or copyrighted audio (V2 Phase 7). That costs some reach, but it is the rule. Make up for it with strong SFX timing (pop/ding on every card), varied kits, and later an approved voice-over.
9. **Loudness:** aim for −14 to −15 LUFS (S3 came out at around −17). If needed, use a two-pass loudnorm or add +2 dB of gain before the limiter.
10. **Trial Reels (owner approved, 7 Nov 2026): 9 + 1 per day.** Each run makes 9 normal Reels + 1 Trial Reel (Metricool `instagramData.type: "TRIAL_REEL"`, autoPublish true). The trial is the day's strongest topic, re-rendered with a **different hook pattern** from the hook bank (its own file `DFM_YYYYMMDD_<topic>_T.mp4`, its own caption). It counts toward the 10-per-day limit and the 45-min gap; post it at least 3 h after the normal version. After 48 h compare views, shares/reach and avg watch time of both versions in `log.md` (section "Trial results") and move the winning hook pattern up in the hook bank. If Metricool rejects TRIAL_REEL, schedule a 10th normal Reel instead and note this in the report.
11. **Brand OS:** keep the same colours, fonts, Yusuf & Maryam and the "This, Not That" stamp across every series. That consistency is our edge over the faceless quote pages.
12. **Posting frequency (owner decision, 7 Nov 2026):** keep 10/day. Every Sunday's run compares average reach and views per Reel over the last 7 days with the week before. If reach per Reel keeps falling for 2 weeks in a row, tell the owner in the report and suggest reducing (e.g. to 5/day). Never reduce without the owner's approval.
